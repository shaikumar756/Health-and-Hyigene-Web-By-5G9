01 · ABOUT THE PROJECT

Small habits. Healthier communities.

Health and hygiene are essential for maintaining well-being and preventing health problems in everyday life.

🧼

Personal Hygiene

Handwashing, bathing, oral care and wearing clean clothes.

💧

Safe Water

Using safe drinking water and keeping it stored in clean, covered containers.

♻️

Clean Surroundings

Responsible waste disposal and maintaining clean community spaces.

02 · PROBLEM IDENTIFICATION

What needs more attention?

01

Improper Waste Disposal

Garbage in open areas can make surroundings dirty and create health risks.

02

Unclean Surroundings

Accumulated waste can attract flies and pests and affect cleanliness.

03

Limited Hygiene Awareness

Awareness about handwashing, safe water and sanitation is important.

03 · HEALTHY PRACTICES

Simple actions, every day.

01

Wash Hands

Use soap and water at important times, especially before eating and after using the toilet.

02

Protect Drinking Water

Keep drinking water clean, covered and safely stored.

03

Maintain Sanitation

Keep toilets and surrounding areas clean and safe.

04

Dispose Waste Properly

Use responsible disposal and segregation practices to keep public spaces clean.
